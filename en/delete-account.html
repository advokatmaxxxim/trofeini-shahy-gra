How to delete your Trophy Chess account

Applies to the web app at trofeini.vn.ua and the Trophy Chess app for Android. Developer: Maksym Prylipa.

This is an English translation of the Ukrainian page Видалення акаунта. If the versions differ, the Ukrainian version prevails.

Option 1. In the game

  1. Open the game and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap “Statistics”.
  3. In the “Account” section, tap “Delete account” and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately.

Option 2. Request by email

If you can’t access the game, write to maxxxim@ukr.net with the subject “Account deletion” from the email address you used to sign in to the game (for Google sign-in – from the address of that Google account). We will delete the account within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • the account together with the email address and sign-in data (including the Google account identifier);
  • the name shown to opponents and the profile;
  • the game archive on the server;
  • ratings and statistics.

What remains

  • Online games you played with other players remain in their archives, but your seat in them becomes empty: these games are no longer linked to you.
  • Service providers’ technical logs are kept for the periods they set and are then deleted automatically.
  • Data on your device (the game archive and settings in browser storage) is deleted when you clear the site data in your browser or uninstall the app.

Deleting some data without deleting the account

If you want to keep your account but delete part of your data, write to maxxxim@ukr.net with the subject “Data deletion” from the email address you used to sign in to the game (for Google sign-in – from the address of that Google account). Say in the email what exactly to delete. We will complete the request within 30 days and let you know.

On request we can delete:

  • the name shown to opponents;
  • individual games or the whole game archive on the server (online games with other players remain in their archives, but are no longer linked to you);
  • ratings and statistics.

The account, the email address and the sign-in data remain (you can’t sign in without them), as do service providers’ technical logs – for the periods they set. You can delete the data on your device yourself: by clearing the site data in your browser or the app’s data in Android settings.

More details are in the Privacy Policy.
