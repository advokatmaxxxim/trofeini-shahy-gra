Trophy Chess Privacy Policy

Version of 26 November 2026. Applies to the web app at trofeini.vn.ua and the Trophy Chess app for Android. The terms of using the game are in the Terms of Use.

This is an English translation of the Ukrainian Політика конфіденційності. If the versions differ, the Ukrainian version prevails.

In short: you can play without a name or email. If you sign in, we store your email, the name you choose, your games and your rating – only to make the game work. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data. You can download or delete your data together with your account at any time, right in the game.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Maksym Prylipa, the developer of Trophy Chess (“we”). Requests about personal data are accepted at maxxxim@ukr.net.

The game is available at trofeini.vn.ua and on Google Play.

2. What data we process

  • Guest account. When you open the game, an anonymous account is created: a technical identifier with no name or email. It is needed to keep your games between sessions. Exceptions are in section 10.
  • Sign-in with Google. The email address, name, profile picture and Google account identifier that Google provides at sign-in (see section 6). We never receive your Google password.
  • Sign-in with an email code. Your email address.
  • Name shown to opponents, which you enter yourself.
  • Games. Moves, time spent on moves, the result and its reason, the rules (Trophy or classical chess), the game mode and time control, the color of your pieces, the computer level, the date of the game, and a random device identifier created by the game itself to tell records from your different devices apart.
  • Rating in games with other players: separately for bullet, blitz and rapid, and separately for Trophy and classical chess.
  • Technical logs kept automatically by the website host and the database server: IP address, time and type of request, browser information.

Age range from Google Play (only in the Android app and only where the law requires app stores to share a user’s age with developers, for example in certain US states): an age range (such as “13–15”) and whether parents have approved changes to the app. Only the app itself uses it, on your device, to decide whether to turn on account sign-in and online games (see section 10). We do not store it – neither on the device nor on the server – and do not share it with anyone.

We do not collect location, contacts or payment data, and we do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.

3. Why we process data and on what basis

  • To provide the game: store your account, games and statistics, sync them across devices, run online games and calculate ratings. Basis: performance of the agreement with you on using the game, which you accept when you start playing.
  • To let you sign in with Google or an email code and use one account on all your devices – at your request, when you tap “Sign in with Google” or “Get a code”. Basis: the same agreement on using the game. You can end this processing by deleting your account.
  • To keep the game reliable and secure: technical logs and protection against abuse. Basis: our legitimate interest in the secure operation of the service.
  • To provide the game in line with laws on users’ age where they apply (age range from Google Play, see section 10). Basis: our legitimate interest in meeting the requirements that apply in your region.

These bases correspond to Article 11 of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” No. 2297-VI of 1 June 2010, and, where the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, GDPR) applies to the processing of your data, to points (b) and (f) of its Article 6(1).

We do not make decisions about you that have legal effects based solely on automated processing. The rating is calculated automatically with the Glicko-2 formula, but it only affects game statistics.

4. Who sees your data

  • Other players see your name shown to opponents and your rating.
  • Online games (moves, result, participants’ names) may be viewed by other players of the game, including guests.
  • Only you see your game archive.
  • Other players do not see your email.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

5. Who helps us process data

On our behalf, data is processed by:

  • Supabase Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) – database, sign-in and real-time online games. The database and the sign-in service are hosted in the Central Europe region we chose (Frankfurt, Germany), and Supabase keeps its service logs in data centers in the EU. The processing terms are set by the Supabase Data Processing Addendum, which is part of its terms of service. Supabase uses its own subprocessors, including its affiliate Supabase, Inc. (USA) for technical support, and hosting providers; Supabase publishes their list on its website: supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/subprocessor-list.
  • Resend, Inc. (USA) – sending emails with sign-in codes. It receives the email address and the text of the email; emails are sent from the European region (Ireland). The processing terms are set by the Resend Data Processing Addendum with standard contractual clauses.

Data is also processed by services we use on their own terms:

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) – hosting of the game website (GitHub Pages). When you open the website, GitHub records and stores your IP address for security purposes in line with its privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Google LLC (USA) – only if you choose to sign in with Google. Sign-in itself takes place on Google’s page under the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play also gives the Android app an age range – only where the law requires it (see section 10).

The main data is stored in Germany. As a member state of the European Economic Area, Germany is recognized under Article 29(3) of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” as ensuring an adequate level of personal data protection.

Some data may also be processed in Singapore and the USA: for example, when Supabase provides technical support, when Resend sends an email with a code, or in GitHub logs. Such transfers are made under Article 29(4) of that Law: they are necessary to perform the agreement with you on using the game (point 2), and we ensure safeguards for your data (point 5) – under its agreement with us, Supabase must comply with the standard contractual clauses approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 and included in its Data Processing Addendum. Resend likewise relies on standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914) included in its Data Processing Addendum. GitHub transfers data to the USA on the basis of the same clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Where the GDPR applies, these are the safeguards provided for in its Chapter V.

6. Data from your Google account

If you sign in with Google, the game requests only basic access – the openid, email and profile scopes. Through this access Google passes to the Supabase sign-in service your email address, name, profile picture and Google account identifier. The game does not request or receive any other data from your Google account (emails, contacts, files, etc.).

  • How we use it: only for sign-in – to recognize your account on all your devices and to show you your email address in the account section. The game does not use the name and picture from your Google profile: you enter the name shown to opponents yourself.
  • Where we store it: in the Supabase sign-in service (Frankfurt region) together with your account, until you delete it.
  • Who we share it with: no one except the processor Supabase listed in section 5. We do not sell data from Google or use it for advertising.

We use data received from Google only as described in this policy and do not review it manually, except when your request to us, the security of the service or the law requires it. Our use and transfer of this data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. What is stored on your device

The game stores in browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) your game archive, settings, an unfinished game, a random device identifier and the sign-in session key. This is necessary for the game to work, including offline. The game does not use third-party cookies for advertising or analytics. Clearing the site data in your browser removes all of this from the device; your account data on the server remains. In the Android app the same data is kept in the app’s own storage on the phone; clearing the app’s data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes it. Android backups do not copy this data.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account data, games and rating – until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete the account itself together with your email address and sign-in data, your profile, game archive and rating. Online games you played with other players remain in their archives, but your seat in them becomes empty: those games are no longer linked to you.
  • Technical logs are kept for the periods set by the providers listed in section 5 and are then deleted automatically.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what data of yours we process, for what purpose and who else has access to it;
  • get a copy of your data – in the game: “Statistics” → “Download my data”;
  • correct inaccurate data – you can change the name shown to opponents in the game; for anything else, write to us;
  • delete your data – in the game: “Statistics” → “Delete account”, or on request by email (see “Delete account”);
  • withdraw consent to processing, where processing is based on consent;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • lodge a complaint with an authority that supervises personal data protection.

These rights are provided by Article 8 of the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection” and, where the GDPR applies, by its Articles 15–21. We reply to requests sent to the email in section 1 within 30 days.

In Ukraine, complaints can be filed with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. Where the GDPR applies, you may contact the data protection supervisory authority of your country (Article 77 GDPR).

10. Children

The game is not intended to collect children’s data, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (including within the meaning of the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA). An account via Google or email may be created by persons aged 14 or older; younger players only with the consent of their parents or other legal guardians. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for processing account data is not consent but the agreement on using the game (section 3), so Article 8 of the GDPR, which concerns a child’s consent, does not apply to it. Nevertheless, for such players we set a higher age: an account may be created independently from the age of 16 or from a lower age at which the law of the player’s country allows a child to consent independently to the processing of data in online services; younger players only with the consent of their parents or other legal guardians. Guest play without signing in is available to everyone: it requires no name or email.

In the Android app, where the law requires app stores to share users’ age with developers (for example in certain US states), the game checks the age range through Google Play before connecting to the server. For players under 13, as well as players whose age Google Play has not verified or whose parents have not approved changes in Google Family Link, account sign-in and online games are unavailable: the game does not even create a guest account for them, and none of their data reaches the server. Playing vs the computer and two-player on one device is available to everyone.

If you believe a child has created an account without proper consent, write to us – we will delete that account.

11. Security

Connections to the game and the server are encrypted (HTTPS), and Supabase stores data on its disks encrypted (AES-256). Access to data in the database is limited by row-level rules: a player sees only their own archive and can change only their own data, and moves, results and ratings are written only by verified server functions. No protection is absolute, but we take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access and will notify you if your data is breached.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, a new version with a new date will appear on this page. We will announce significant changes in the game before they take effect.

13. Contact

For any questions about personal data, write to maxxxim@ukr.net.
